THE GREEN ENCYCLOPEDIA
======================
Created by Kenji Santacruz
Shades sourced from colorguide.org. Built with AI assistance.

A book-style encyclopedia of 1,410 named shades of green in 13 sections,
plus tools to discover new greens and keep your own collection.


GETTING STARTED
---------------
1. Save green-encyclopedia.html anywhere on your computer.
2. Double-click it. It opens in your web browser.

Nothing needs to be installed and no internet connection is needed.
The book reopens on the page you last had open.


READING THE BOOK
----------------
Each shade has its own two-page spread: a colour sample on the left, and
its Name, HEX, RGB and HSL values on the right.

  Turn pages      Previous / Next buttons, the left and right arrow keys,
                  or a swipe on a touch screen.
  Jump to a page  Type a number in the "Page" box at the bottom.
  First / last    Home and End keys.
  Copy a value    Click "Copy" beside any HEX, RGB or HSL value.

Each section opens with a page showing every shade in it as a tile.
Click a tile to jump straight to that shade.


TABLE OF CONTENTS
-----------------
The Contents panel on the left lists every section. Use the "Contents"
button at the top to show or hide it.

  Click a section name      Jump to the start of that section.
  Click the small arrow     Expand or collapse the list of its shades.
  Click a shade name        Jump to that shade's page.
  Search box                Find shades by name or hex code.

The sections, in order: Basics, Very Light Green, Light Green, Soft Green,
Pastel Green, Medium Green, Bright Green, Neon Green, Deep Green,
Natural Green, Neutral Green, Dark Green, Very Dark Green.
These built-in sections and shades cannot be changed.


DISCOVER A SHADE
----------------
Open "Discover a shade" (top of the window) to see a randomly generated
green. A new one appears every time you open the page, or when you press
"Show me another".

To keep one, optionally type a name and press "Save to My Shades".
If you leave the name blank, the hex code is used as the name.


ADD YOUR OWN
------------
Open "Add your own" and type a colour in any of these forms:

  HEX   #2E8B57
  RGB   rgb(46, 139, 87)
  HSL   hsl(146, 50%, 36%)

A live preview appears. Optionally name it, then press "Add to My Shades".

Two notes may appear. Neither stops you from adding the shade:
  - The colour does not look very green.
  - The exact same shade already exists, with the name and section (or
    My Shades subsection) where it can be found. This only appears for an
    exact match, never for a shade that is merely close. The same note
    appears on the Discover page.

If you have created subsections (see below), both pages also show a
"Subsection" menu so a new shade can go straight into one.


MY SHADES
---------
The last section of the book holds everything you save or add.

On a saved shade's page:
  Rename                    Change its name at any time.
  Move earlier / later      Change its position.
  Subsection menu           Move it into a subsection, or out of one.
  Remove from my shades     Delete it.

Reordering by dragging:
  Drag shade names up or down under My Shades in the Contents panel, or
  drag the tiles on the My Shades page.


SUBSECTIONS
-----------
Subsections are optional folders inside My Shades. If you never create
one, all your shades simply sit in My Shades.

  Create            "New subsection" on the My Shades page, or
                    "New subsection..." in a shade's subsection menu.
  Add shades        Drag a shade onto the subsection's name in the
                    Contents panel, or use the shade's subsection menu.
  Take a shade out  Drag it onto "My Shades", or choose "No subsection".
  Collapse          Click the small arrow beside the subsection's name.
  Reorder           Drag subsection names in the Contents panel, or use
                    Move up / Move down on the subsection's page.
  Rename            On the subsection's page.
  Sort              The "Sort..." menu orders the shades once, lightest to
                    darkest, darkest to lightest, or by hue. You can still
                    drag them afterwards.
  Delete            "Delete subsection" removes the folder only; its
                    shades stay in My Shades.
                    "Delete subsection and its shades" removes both.

Page order in the book: shades with no subsection come first, then each
subsection in turn.


SELECTING SEVERAL SHADES
------------------------
This works on shade names under My Shades in the Contents panel, and on
the tiles on the My Shades and subsection pages.

  Shift-click     Click one shade, then Shift-click another to select
                  everything between them. This stays inside one
                  subsection.
  Ctrl-click      Add or remove single shades from the selection, across
                  any subsections. On a Mac, use Command-click.
  Whole group     Shift-click or Ctrl-click a subsection's name, or press
                  "Select all" on the subsection's page.
  Clear           Press Esc, press "Clear", or click any shade normally.

With shades selected you can:
  Remove them     "Remove" in the Contents panel, or the Delete key.
                  A prompt lists what will be removed and waits for OK.
  Move them       Drag any selected shade and the rest follow, or use the
                  "Move to..." menu in the Contents panel.


UNDO
----
After removing shades, sorting, or deleting a subsection, an "Undo" button
appears at the bottom of the window for about 12 seconds. It restores
everything to the arrangement it had before. Only the most recent action
can be undone.


BACKING UP AND MOVING YOUR SHADES
---------------------------------
Your shades are stored inside the web browser you use to open the file,
on that computer. They are not stored inside the HTML file itself.

This means:
  - Opening the file in a different browser, or on another computer,
    starts with an empty My Shades.
  - Clearing your browser's site data can erase My Shades.

To keep them safe or move them, use the buttons on the My Shades page:
  Export backup   Saves my-green-shades.json, containing your shades,
                  their names, their order and your subsections.
  Import backup   Loads a backup file. You can replace what you have, or
                  add the file's shades to it.

Exporting a backup from time to time is recommended.


USING IT ON AN IPHONE OR IPAD
-----------------------------
Tapping the file in Files, Mail or Messages opens a preview that does not
run the encyclopedia. You will see a short notice instead of the book.
This is a limit of the iPhone's file preview, not a fault in the file.

To use it on an iPhone or iPad, do one of these:
  - Put the file on a website (any free static web host works) and open
    that web address in Safari. You can then add it to your Home Screen.
  - Open the file with an HTML viewer app from the App Store.

Backups on a phone or tablet: "Export backup" opens a panel showing the
backup as text. Copy it (or use "Share or save as a file" where offered)
and keep it in a note or an email to yourself. "Import backup" lets you
choose a backup file or paste that text back in. Backups work in both
directions between phone and computer.

On a touch screen: swipe to turn pages, and use the Move buttons, the
subsection menus and "Select all" in place of dragging, Shift-click and
Ctrl-click.


TIPS
----
  - On phones and tablets dragging may not work. Use the Move buttons and
    the subsection menus instead.
  - A current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari is recommended.
  - Some shades appear in more than one built-in section, because the
    source lists them that way.
